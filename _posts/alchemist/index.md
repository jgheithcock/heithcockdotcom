## Syrups

Avoid boiling the fruit in the syrup as it can create a scum you will need to remove, but more importantly, will boil away the delicate fruit aromas you are wanting to capture. This is especially important for lavender syrup as it can make for a bitter, soapy tasting syrup. In all cases, you will want to use equal parts sugar to water by weight. 1 cup of sugar weighs approximately 7/8 cup of water.

</section>

### Lavender Syrup

<section>

#### To Make
Equal parts water and white sugar by weight, simmered until all sugar is dissolved (you can also try the cold technique which involves lots of shaking). Let cool then add a cup of [lavender flowers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08X6T6B2G) to 2-3 cups of syrup. Let steep for a day, then strain out flowers.
</section>

<section>

#### Uses
Goes great in coffee and tea. Can be used in sweetening [Instant Lemonade](#instant-lemonade) or a [Gin Old Fashioned](#gin-old-fashioned).
</section>

### Cinnamon Brown Sugar Syrup
<section>

#### To Make
Simmer brown sugar and water together with cinnamon sticks, and optionally a few allspice berries and a clove. After bringing it to a simmer, cover pot and let sit as many hours as you can wait (overnight is great).
</section>

<section>

#### Uses
Great in coffee, cocoa, hot chocolate and hundreds of cocktails. Mix with two parts unsweetened, white grapefruit juice to make [Don's Mix #3](https://thelosttikilounge.com/ingredients/dons-secret-recipes/#wprm-recipe-container-3468), famous for his zombies. Or [make my version](#zombie). You can also make my [Mark Two](#mark-two).
</section>

### Apricot Syrup
<section>

#### To Make
Simmer 7 ounces of dried fruit in 2 cups of water with 1 cup of sugar (and optionally a cinnamon stick) for about a half an hour. Let cool and optionally add the juice from a lemon. Strain out juice and use fruit paste to make [fried pies](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HlI_0eIGtCocBr09Yhp9A3a8Z5tgW7Xmv1RhgM-qiIo) or use on pancakes, waffles or toast.
</section>

<section>

#### Uses
Aside from sweetening tea, makes a great [Zombie](#zombie).
</section>

## Tinctures and Bitters
### Lemon Tincture
<section>
A Tincture is just the extracted fruit, without any bittering agent. I made a Lemon Bitters at the same time as making the tincture but I prefer the pure Lemon essence.

#### To Make
Use a vegetable peeler to shave a dozen lemons. Normally you would want to be careful to avoid the pith, the white spongy part as it can make your bake bitter - but hey! the more the merrier. Fill a mason jar and add vodka to cover. Keep closed and shake vigorously every day for a month or so. Tip: After decanting via a strainer, run the mix through a coffee filter after a few days to get the rest of the solids to avoid a film.

</section>
<section>

#### Uses

So many uses! Aside from [Instant Lemonade](#instant-lemonade), or just adding to anything in place of some lemon zest, you can also make my [Smoke and Embers](#smoke-and-embers).

</section>

### Cherry Bitters
<section>

#### To Make
Store as many fresh, pitted, Washington cherries (2 cups) as will fit in a mason jar, add one anise star, a couple of cinnamon sticks, some allspice berries, one juniper berry, a split open vanilla pod, some black tea (two tea bags). Cover with bourbon. Shake daily for about a month. Decant and, as a above, then filter through a coffee filter. Tip: Do not go longer than a month or you will, as I did, develop a bit of a raisiny flavor

(For a different recipe, see this [online version](https://www.seriouseats.com/homemade-cherry-bitters-angostura-substitute-recipe).) 
</section>
<section>

#### Uses
Can be used in place of regular Angostura bitters in general (try it in a Manhatten) or a [Zombie](#zombie). Key ingredient in my [Mark Two](#mark-two).

</section>


### Lavender Bitters
<section>

#### To Make
Fill jar with [lavender flowers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08X6T6B2G), cover with vodka. In a separate, smaller jar, make a tea of the bittering agents. You can use a wide variety of things but this one used: Burdock root, Barberry root, Black Walnut Leaf, Dried Orange Peel, Milk Thistle, Fringetree Bark and a small amount of Quassia. I made about half. Shake both daily for about a month. After decanting and filtering, add as much of the bitter tea as you want. To taste, have a small shot glass with some cold, but not ice cold water, add a tsp of the lavender increasing amounts of the bitter tea. (Confession: I added them all.)
</section>
<section>

#### Uses
Lavender bitters gives that [Instant Lemonade] a nice zip as a bonus to the lemon tincture. I also use it in my [Titanic](#titanic).
</section>


## Recipes
<section>

### Gin Old fashioned
3 parts gin to 1 part [lavender syrup](#lavender-syrup) with a tsp of [lavender bitters](#lavender-bitters). Shake over ice and serve plain up or over ice in an old fashioned glass.
</section>
<section>

### Instant Lemonade
Mix 1 C of water with 2 T of [lavender syrup](#lavender-syrup) with 1 tsp [lemon tincture](#lemon-tincture) and optionally 1 tsp lavender bitters](#lavender-bitters). Shake over ice and serve.
</section>
<section>

### Mark Two
3 parts bourbon (I use Makers Mark) with 1 part [cinnamon brown sugar syrup](#cinnamon-brown-sugar-syrup) and 1 tsp [cherry bitters](#cherry-bitters). Shake over ice and serve in an old-fashioned glass.
</section>
<section>

### Smoke and Embers
3 parts Tennessee whiskey (Jack Daniels) with with 1 part [cinnamon brown sugar syrup](#cinnamon-brown-sugar-syrup) and 1 tsp [lemon tincture](#lemon-tincture). I also add a few dashes of [barrel aged whiskey bitters](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001N0WDKQ) from Fee brothers but that isn't included!
</section>
<section>

### Titanic
This is a dressed up version of the [Gin old fashioned](#gin-old-fashioned) above.

- 2 oz Empress Gin, 
- 1/2 oz [lavender syrup](#lavender-syrup)
- 3/4 oz [Pama Pomegranate](https://www.totalwine.com/liqueurs-cordials-schnapps/fruit/pama-pomegranate-liqueur-375ml/p/15570375-1)
- 3/4 oz [Creme de Violette](https://www.totalwine.com/liqueurs-cordials-schnapps/herbal-spice/rothman-winter-creme-de-violette-liqueur-750ml/p/103104750-1)
- 7 drops [Scrappy's Fire bitters](https://www.totalwine.com/mixers-water-soda/syrups/scrappy-s-firewater/p/182439233-1)
- 2 oz Rose lemonade
- juice from half of a lime.

Shake all over ice and garnish with a lime wedge.
<section>

### Zombie
This is adapted from the [Spievek Zombie](https://cocktailvirgin.blogspot.com/2016/08/spievak-zombie.html), preserved by Beachbum Berry, found in Louis Spievak's Barbecue Chef.

Equal parts (1 oz by default) of 151 Rum, Gold Rum, Light Rum, [cinnamon brown sugar syrup](#cinnamon-brown-sugar-syrup), [apricot syrup](#apricot-syrup), Lime, Lemon and Pineapple juice. A good tsp or two of the [cherry bitters](#cherry-bitters). Shake and serve over ice.
</section>
